# SEO + AI Search — Full analysis and plan (2026-10-29)

Sources: Google Search Console (`sc-domain:sicoesmonitor.com`, Findable), Findable site audit (45 pages), Findable domain/keyword/backlink data (Bolivia, es), live SERP checks, web-grounded AI-answer probes. Market = Bolivia.

## 1. Where we are

| Metric | 2026-10-02 baseline | 2026-10-29 |
|---|---|---|
| Real clicks / day (GSC) | ~5 (Sep 1 – Oct 4) | **~20** (Oct 12–28, 17 days ≈ 336 clicks) |
| Findable ranking keywords | 34 | 45–46 |
| Indexed pages with impressions | ~19 | 54 URLs in GSC |
| Referring domains | n/a | 45 — **all spam** (see §3.5) |
| Site-audit issues | titles >60 chars ×24 | 0 critical; 4 long meta descriptions, `/login` has no H1 (noindex, ignore) |

Competitors: **infosiscon.com ≈ 31,000 organic visits/mo, 603 keywords** (us ≈ 1,100 est. / ~600 real clicks per month); sicoeshoy.bo ≈ 1,960 visits, 24 keywords.

> Caution on impressions: they jumped to 7–10k/day on Oct 26–28, almost all on the head term "sicoes" (≈35k impressions / 2 weeks). Spikes like these usually include automated rank-checkers. **Clicks are the reliable metric.**

## 2. What is working
- Informational guides rank on page 1 within days: ANPE (pos 4.0), DBC (4.3), errores (4.2), contratación menor (5.2), modalidades (5.0).
- **Personnel queries convert best** (CTR 2.5–7%): city hub 45 clicks, personal guide 42, "sicoes requerimiento de personal 2026 la paz" 5.6% CTR. SERP for these is weak (Infosiscon entity pages + Facebook posts).
- Location pages earn real clicks: La Paz 55, Santa Cruz 33, Cochabamba 19, El Alto 18, Oruro 14.
- Technical health is clean: 45/45 pages indexable, in sitemap, no broken links, fast responses.

## 3. Diagnosis — what to fix

1. **Head term "sicoes" (301k/mo): we sit at #8 with ~0 clicks.** SERP above us: official portal, 2 Facebook pages, video, MEFP manual PDF, sicoeshoy guide. Page 1 positions 1–6 are the only ones that click. Same pattern for "rupe" (3.9k impressions, pos 7.5, 4 clicks) and "sigep" (1.1k, pos 10).
2. **Our data coverage is tiny, so many location pages were empty.** The scraper searches ~35 keywords and reads only the first result page of each (≈144 tenders). Santa Cruz — a top-clicked page — listed **0 tenders**; Oruro, Tarija, Chuquisaca, Beni, Potosí, 4 of 8 category pages likewise (audit: ~230 words). Infosiscon shows thousands.
3. **Thin/duplicate location pages** (same intro text everywhere).
4. **No unmet-intent coverage** for RUPE login/certificate, SIGEP beneficiary (14.8k/mo "sigep beneficiario"), consultor individual de línea, Compro Hecho en Bolivia / Mercado Virtual.
5. **Backlinks: 43 links / 45 domains, 100% automated spam** (PBN, "tiered link building" anchors, casino footprints, spam score 60–75). Zero legitimate authority links. This is why we can't climb from #8 to top 5 on head terms.
6. AI-answer probe: we are cited for "qué es SICOES", "alertas de licitaciones", RUPE; we were **not** cited for ANPE, RUPE-vs-SIGEP, "cómo postular" (now covered).
7. Tooling gaps: Lighthouse failed on all 20 pages in Findable (tool issue, no data); rank tracker run fails on a Findable billing error; GA4 not connected (no conversion data).

## 4. Shipped in this iteration
- **Scraper coverage**: +33 place-name keywords, +~60 broad procurement keywords, parse of the portal's initial listing, 22-minute time budget so the DB write always runs. (⚠ untested against sicoes.gob.bo from here — see §6.)
- **Location pages**: unique per-department and per-municipality content (capital, kinds of entities, public universities, tips), per-place FAQ + FAQPage schema, **national fallback table** instead of an empty box, municipio pages `noindex` only when the query succeeded and returned nothing.
- **4 new guides** (2,600–3,100 words): Certificado RUPE (generar/verificar/ingresar), Registro de beneficiario SIGEP, Consultor individual de línea, Compro Hecho en Bolivia / Mercado Virtual / Subasta.
- Pillar page "¿Qué es SICOES?" gets a goal→guide map linking all 20 other guides (target: head term "sicoes").
- Trimmed long meta descriptions; optional `docs/disavow-sicoesmonitor.txt` (43 spam domains).
- Total: **21 guides**, ~49 URLs in sitemap, `llms-full.txt` and RSS updated automatically.

## 6. Risks and limits
- The scraper changes could not be run against sicoes.gob.bo from this environment (network blocked). They reuse the existing, proven search function and add a time budget; if the Actions log shows problems, revert `scripts/scrape-sicoes.ts` only.
- Legal figures in guides come from public references (D.S. 0181, guides); have a procurement-law reviewer check them before promoting. The "RUPE obligatorio > Bs 20.000" statement is attributed to SICOES's guide and flagged to verify.
- Regulations change: re-verify thresholds and portal sections every quarter.
